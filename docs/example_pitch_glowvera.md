## Creative guidance

- **Open with one of:** demo, challenge, story. The predicted difference among these is small, so test two of them.
- **Do not open with a plain sponsor introduction** ("this video is sponsored by..."). The model puts it 24% below the best hook in beauty.
- **Put the call to action in the middle of the video**, not the end.
- **Book each creator once in this window** and avoid creators with 3 or more deals in the past 30 days.

Starter opening lines to adapt (the creator must only keep claims that are true for them):

- demo: "Watch how fast this comes together with Glowvera."
- challenge: "I used only Glowvera for seven days. Here is what happened."
- story: "Last month I almost gave up on beauty, and then Glowvera changed that."

## What to expect (model estimate)

- Typical deal ROAS with this mix and creative: **2.31** (median outcome; the average is usually higher because a few deals do very well).
- The same creative on Monk's historical tier mix for beauty: 1.75. The mix change is worth +32%.
- This mix with a plain sponsor opening and the CTA at the end: 0.85. The creative changes are worth +170%.
- Rough revenue at the median: Rs 22.5 lakh on Rs 9.7 lakh spend.

These come from a model trained on simulated data. Real results will differ; the CTA effect in particular is likely smaller in real campaigns. Treat this as a starting plan and measure it.
